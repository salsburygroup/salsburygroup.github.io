Our research asks how molecular-scale structure and motion give rise to biological function. The descriptions below reflect established, publicly documented areas of work rather than confidential or unpublished projects.

<div class="research-stack">
  <article class="research-panel">
    <p class="eyebrow">Molecular simulation</p>
    <h2>Conformational dynamics across time and scale</h2>
    <p>Molecular dynamics provides an atom-by-atom view of biomolecular motion. We use simulations to study structural ensembles, binding interactions, mutation effects, and slow or rare changes that may be difficult to isolate experimentally.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="research-panel">
    <p class="eyebrow">Allostery & regulation</p>
    <h2>How information moves through proteins</h2>
    <p>Binding at one site can reshape dynamics far away. We analyze correlated motions, interaction networks, and shifts in conformational populations to understand allosteric regulation in proteins and molecular complexes.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="research-panel">
    <p class="eyebrow">Methods & data science</p>
    <h2>Turning trajectories into reliable evidence</h2>
    <p>Long simulations produce large, high-dimensional datasets. We develop and apply statistical and machine-learning methods to assess sampling, identify informative coordinates, and distinguish persistent molecular behavior from noise.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="research-panel">
    <p class="eyebrow">Molecular discovery</p>
    <h2>Connecting physical insight to disease biology</h2>
    <p>In collaboration with experimental researchers, we apply computational biophysics to questions in drug discovery, cancer biology, protein aggregation, DNA damage and repair, and molecular recognition. Public descriptions remain broad until findings are peer reviewed.</p>
  </article>
</div>

## How we work

<div class="process-row">
  <div><span>01</span><strong>Frame</strong><p>Start with a precise biological question and an explicit physical model.</p></div>
  <div><span>02</span><strong>Simulate</strong><p>Choose methods and sampling strategies suited to the time and length scales.</p></div>
  <div><span>03</span><strong>Validate</strong><p>Test convergence, sensitivity, and consistency with available evidence.</p></div>
  <div><span>04</span><strong>Interpret</strong><p>Translate molecular behavior into mechanisms and testable hypotheses.</p></div>
