The Salsbury Group contributes computational biophysics expertise to the [Structural Biology and Drug Discovery Shared Resource](https://school.wakehealth.edu/research/institutes-and-centers/comprehensive-cancer-center/shared-resources-and-cores/structural-biology-and-drug-discovery) of the Atrium Health Wake Forest Baptist Comprehensive Cancer Center. Freddie Salsbury serves as a Co-Director of the resource.

## From structural questions to testable models

The Shared Resource connects investigators with complementary expertise in structural biology, computational chemistry, molecular modeling, and medicinal chemistry. Computational work can support projects ranging from basic questions about biomolecular structure and dynamics to structure-guided discovery.

## Good points to begin a conversation

- A biomolecular structure, interaction, mutation, or mechanism that needs quantitative study
- A structure-based screening or compound-prioritization question
- A grant or pilot project that needs a realistic computational plan
- Existing structural or simulation data that need careful analysis and interpretation

The official Shared Resource page is the authoritative source for current capabilities, access, reservations, and Cancer Center acknowledgment language.
