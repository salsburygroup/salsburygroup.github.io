Students in my FYS 100 course, *AI and the Physics of Life: AlphaFold and Why Molecular Shape Matters*, visited Wake Forest's high-performance computing touchdown space and data center on August 26.

We began with a short introduction to how a computing cluster differs from a single machine. Students then toured the data center and saw the server racks and network connections used for computational research and teaching.

The course connects AI methods such as AlphaFold to molecular shape and physical principles. The visit gave the class a look at the machines that run large calculations.

Thanks to Wake Forest's IS/HPC staff for hosting us.
